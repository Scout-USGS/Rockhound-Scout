Before your first scan

Rockhound Scout finds promising spots from geologic, road and stream maps. Nobody has visited or checked these spots.

It does not check property lines, road right-of-way, collecting rules, or safety. Roadside right-of-way may end at or near the pavement, nearby land is often private, and creek banks and bridges can be steep or unstable.

How to search

Just type it the way you’d tell a friend. Only a place is required.

Pick a place

  • A town: near Mount Ida, AR
  • A hotel, landmark or address works too
  • We search about 20 miles around it — unless you specify

Narrow it down

  • Distance: within 10 miles of Glendive, MT
  • A road: on Hwy 101 or on FM 306
  • A direction: south of Venice, FL
  • Creeks: creek crossings near Keokuk, IA

Say what you’re after (optional)

  • Herkimer diamonds near Herkimer, NY
  • shark teeth, agate, geodes, petrified wood, fossils…
  • Not sure? Ask what can I find near…

Kinds of hotspot cards

  • Crossing — where a creek or river passes under the road. Stop at the bridge and check the gravel bars and banks. One target spot.
  • Stretch — a length of road that runs across rock known to produce what you’re after. There’s no single spot: drive it slowly from the green pin to the red pin and check road cuts, bar ditches and small washes along the way.
  • Contact — where the road passes from one rock formation into another. The change can be anywhere in a short stretch, so check both shoulders near the marked spot.
  • Shore — a lake, river or beach shoreline where waves and water sort out loose stones.
  • ⭐ Known site / 💲 Pay dig — well-known collecting spots and fee digs.

⚙ Filters

  • Include pay-to-dig sites adds fee digs and commercial mines (marked 💲)
  • Freestyle skips the well-known sites so you get less-visited spots. You can also just type freestyle in your search.
  • Skip road stretches leaves out Stretch cards, so every card is a spot you can stop at.

Good to know

  • A scan takes a minute or two
  • A scan that errors or finds no hotspots isn’t counted
  • Tap any example under the button to try it, or describe your target area
